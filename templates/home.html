The Challenge: Gender Gap in Digital Finance

Despite the rise of decentralized finance, women — especially in emerging economies — are being left behind. Key barriers include:

  • Limited Access to Education — Few tailored, beginner-friendly resources on crypto & blockchain
  • Heightened Risk Perception — Fear of scams, volatility, and complex technology
  • Underrepresentation — Very few visible female role models and mentors in Web3
  • Missed Economic Opportunity — Risk of exclusion from new income, investment & entrepreneurship paths
Our Goals
  • Build foundational knowledge of cryptocurrency, blockchain & DeFi
  • Enable participants to securely create & manage crypto wallets + send test transactions
  • Drastically improve ability to detect and avoid crypto scams
  • Showcase real career, freelancing & entrepreneurial paths in Web3
  • Create a supportive peer network + connect to mentors
Target: 80–100 young women aged 18–30
Day 1 – Demystifying the Blockchain
  • → What is money? From barter to Bitcoin
  • → Core concepts: Blockchain, decentralization, cryptography (simplified)
  • → Major cryptocurrencies: Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins
  • → Hands-on: Exploring real blockchains using explorers
Day 2 – Hands-On Crypto & Security First
  • → Wallets: Custodial vs non-custodial, hot vs cold
  • → Practical: Set up MetaMask (or similar), secure seed phrase
  • → Send & receive small test transactions (covered by project funds)
  • → Masterclass: Identify phishing, rug pulls, fake giveaways
  • → Best practices: Private key safety, secure social media habits
Day 3 – Building a Future in Web3
  • → Overview: DeFi, NFTs, DAOs, Metaverse
  • → Real African use-cases relevant to women
  • → Career Panel: Women already working in blockchain (devs, community managers, freelancers, investors)
  • → Next steps: Learning resources, Discord/Telegram communities, freelance platforms
