Everything you need to know about the Women in Crypto Initiative

Empowering Women in East Africa

The Women in Crypto Initiative is a program designed to empower 80-100 young women aged 18-30 in Kenya with blockchain, cryptocurrency, and Web3 skills. Our 3-day intensive workshop covers everything from blockchain basics to practical wallet management and career opportunities in the crypto space.

Location: Nairobi / Kajiado County, Kenya

The pilot workshop is scheduled for June 2024. Exact dates will be communicated to selected participants via email. The workshop runs for 3 consecutive days:

  • Day 1: Demystifying Blockchain
  • Day 2: Hands-On Crypto & Security
  • Day 3: Building a Future in Web3

Registration is simple and free! Follow these steps:

  1. Click the "Register Now" button on the homepage
  2. Fill in your personal information
  3. Select your participant category
  4. Upload a valid ID proof (National ID, Passport, or Driver's License)

No experience needed!

Our program is designed for complete beginners. We'll guide you through:

  • Basic blockchain concepts in simple terms
  • Step-by-step wallet setup
  • Practical exercises with test transactions
  • Security best practices

All you need is basic computer literacy and enthusiasm to learn!
