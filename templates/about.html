About Us

Empowering the next generation of women leaders in blockchain technology

Our Mission

To bridge the digital financial gender gap in East Africa by providing young women with accessible, practical education in blockchain technology and cryptocurrencies.

Our Vision

A future where women in East Africa are equal participants and leaders in the global digital economy, leveraging blockchain technology for financial independence.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, the Women in Crypto Initiative was born from a simple observation: women in East Africa are being left behind in the blockchain revolution.

While cryptocurrency adoption grows across the continent, women face unique barriers to entry including limited access to education, heightened risk perception, and underrepresentation in the space.

We decided to change that. Our pilot program in Nairobi and Kajiado County aims to empower 80-100 young women with the knowledge and skills they need to participate safely and confidently in the crypto economy.
